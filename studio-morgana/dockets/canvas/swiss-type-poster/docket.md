name: Swiss type poster
niche: canvas
kinds: poster, event post, announcement, quote post
size: 1080 by 1350 (works from 4:5 to 2:3; for 16:9 put the headline left and the circle right)
tags: typographic, grid, bold, minimal, two-colour, international style
use when: one strong phrase of one to three short words per line carries the piece; events, talks, launches, manifestos; no photo needed

## Look
A pale ground, one enormous condensed headline stacked flush left, one flat coloured circle behind it, and small
monospaced labels held between two hairline rules. Everything hangs on one left edge.

## Grid
- Margin 72 on a 1080 width (6.7%) on every side; everything starts at x 72 or ends at x 1008.
- Top rule at y 150 (11%), bottom rule at y 1180 (87%); labels sit 30 above the top rule and 40 below the bottom one.
- Headline baselines at 41%, 61% and 80% of the height: equal steps of 260 (0.87 of the font size), so the lines nearly touch.
- The circle's centre is right of centre and above the middle (70%, 35%). "520 big" draws it about 1040 across, so it
  spans most of the width and bleeds off the top and right edges; its lower-left edge cuts through the headline.

## Type
- Headline: Big Shoulders Display, size 300 (28% of the width), all capitals, aligned left, one word per line, three lines at most.
- Labels: Martian Mono, size 20 (1.9% of the width), all capitals, tracking 4, aligned left, ink; the date aligned right on the right edge.
- Only these two fonts. No bold, no italics, no third size.

## Palette
Roles: ground, ink, accent (the circle and nothing else).
- Bone #efe9df, ink #111111, signal red #e63b2e (the example).
- Paper #f4f1ea, ink #0e1a2b, cobalt #2546d8.
- Black #0d0d0d, bone #efe9df as ink, acid #d7ff3a (inverted: dark ground, light type).

## Image
None. If the brief insists on a photo, put it inside the circle, duotone in ink and accent; the circle stays the only colour.

## Materials
Flat ("Let there be a flat canvas"): this look is print, exact colours, no light.

## Signature
The headline crosses the circle: letters run over the accent so the two layers lock together. Never move the circle
clear of the type.

## Don't
- Centre anything. Everything is flush left on the margin (only the date is flush right).
- Add a gradient, a shadow, a second accent colour or a second display font.
- Let the headline run past x 1008 or put more than about seven letters on a line at size 300; shorten the words or lower the size.
- Put labels anywhere but between the rules' edges and the page edge.

## Slots
Change: the headline words (one per line), the kicker (top label), the topics and the date (bottom labels), the palette
(pick one of the three or keep the roles), the circle's position along the right half.
Keep: the margin, the rules, the fonts, the stacking, the one accent.

## How we did it
- Lines 3–7: name the palette roles (bone, ink, signal) and paint the ground; the label style carries font, size,
  tracking, alignment and colour so every label matches.
- Lines 9–10: the circle first, so it sits behind the type.
- Lines 12–15: three Write lines, one word each, at x 72 and baselines 560, 820, 1080, all ink.
- Lines 17–19: two hairline rules (936 by 3) spanning margin to margin.
- Lines 21–23: the labels in the label style; the date aligned right at x 1008.
